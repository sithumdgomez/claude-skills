# Decisions

The owner's judgement calls, newest at the bottom. One line each, dated.
Row-level and payee-level answers live in `config/decisions.csv` (written by
`apply_review.py`); this file is for the calls that explain them.

Format: `YYYY-MM-DD - decision - why`

- (example) 2026-10-02 - Transfers to/from "A NGUYEN" are loans - friend, repays within weeks
- (example) 2026-10-02 - Mobile phone is Mixed, 60% business - accountant to confirm
